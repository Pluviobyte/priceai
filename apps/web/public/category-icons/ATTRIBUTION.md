# Category icons

Marks for the catalogue categories on the channel page.

## Composed from brand marks

- `mail.svg` — Outlook and Gmail overlaid and tilted into one mark. The shelf holds
  Outlook, Gmail, iCloud and education mailboxes at once, so no single vendor's logo can
  stand for it; the two largest are shown together instead. Drawn here from the two
  well-known marks rather than copied from a vendor asset.

## Drawn here, not official marks

- `video.svg` — a seedling above an open gold-and-green ring. It follows the seed-and-
  growth motif Seedance is known by, but it is our own drawing: no licence-clean official
  Seedance vector exists (lobe-icons carries ByteDance and Doubao, not Seedance, and
  seedance.ai is a third-party aggregator, not ByteDance). The shelf also holds Runway,
  可灵 and Adobe Firefly, so stamping any one vendor's logo on it would misattribute.
- `verification.svg` — a phone with a code on its screen, in the convention the 接码 sites
  themselves use: sms-act.net's mark is a red-pink handset on a dark ground with content
  on the display, and lubansms.com likewise illustrates the service with handsets.
- `design.svg`, `domestic.svg`, `other.svg` — neutral glyphs. 设计办公 spans Canva, Figma
  and Notion; 国产模型 spans DeepSeek and 豆包; 其他 spans a dozen unrelated goods. None
  has a single owner whose mark could represent it.

The brand names and marks belong to their respective owners and are used here only to
identify the goods a category holds.

Colour marks carry no `prefers-color-scheme` rule: their palettes read on either theme.
The remaining outline glyphs do carry one, since a single-colour stroke would otherwise
disappear against a dark background — they are loaded through `<img>` and cannot inherit
the page's text colour.
