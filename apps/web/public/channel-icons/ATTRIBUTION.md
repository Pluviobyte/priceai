# Channel icons

Marks for the purchase channels a price can come from, used to identify the store a
listed price was read at.

- `apple.svg` — Apple mark, path from Simple Icons (CC0 1.0). https://simpleicons.org
- `google.svg` — Google mark, the four-colour G in its standard geometry.

The brand names and marks belong to their respective owners and are used here only to
identify the channel each price was collected from.

Both files carry a `prefers-color-scheme` rule so the mark stays legible on either theme;
they are loaded through `<img>`, which resolves that against the viewer's own setting.
